Step 1. Systematic literature review

  • Venues: main tracks of NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, AAAI, IJCAI
  • Period: January 2024 to April 2026
  • Excluded: workshop, findings and demo papers
  • Keywords: derived from the top-10 Google Scholar results for "AI scientist" using KeyBERT keyphrase extraction, then consolidated into three groups:
    • automated / automatic / autonomous scientific discovery
    • automated / automatic / autonomous scientific research
    • AI scientist

15 candidate papers, 11 relevant after screening, covering 19 distinct tools.
