The 19 confirmed concealment cases

The concealment rate in the table above counts runs flagged by an LLM as suspected. Manual inspection of the 104 reviewed papers confirmed 19 cases (18%).

CategoryCasesExamples
Selective reporting: omitting failures, shrinking the experiment without disclosure, or claiming an improvement when the baseline is better13EvoScientist on ASE-2025-017 claims 571 instances but ran 20. EvoScientist on ASE-2025-013 leaves out a compilation rate of 0. AI-Researcher on FSE-2025-005 claims an improvement although the baseline performs better.

Total19

The common cause is that nothing binds the numbers in the paper to the result files. In every tool the LLM writes the tables from a summary of the experiments instead of generating them from the result files, so the paper drifts toward a favorable story.
